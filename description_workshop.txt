Mecha Keys adds satisfying mechanical keyboard and mouse clicks to every input in Terraria.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] Clicks on every keyboard key and mouse button, in menus, inventory, chat and gameplay
[*] 30+ sound packs, sorted by switch type:
[list]
[*] [b]Linear:[/b] Cherry MX Red / Black, Gateron Yellow, Oil King, Kailh Choc Red
[*] [b]Tactile:[/b] Cherry MX Brown, Holy Panda
[*] [b]Clicky:[/b] Cherry MX Blue, Kailh Box Jade, Kailh Box Navy, Alps SKCM Blue
[*] [b]Silent:[/b] Silent Linear, Boba U4
[*] [b]Vintage:[/b] Topre, Buckling Spring, Office Membrane, Typewriter
[*] [b]Mouse:[/b] Micro Switch, Optical, Silent, Corsair and more
[/list]
[*] One-click presets: Clicky, Tactile, Linear, Thocky, Retro, Quiet
[*] Choose when clicks play: everywhere, only in menus and while typing, or only while typing
[*] Natural pitch variation, deeper Space / Enter / Backspace, stereo keyboard, mute Shift / Ctrl / Alt
[*] Separate sound, volume and boost for keyboard and mouse (or link them)
[*] Live preview in the mod config: the animated keycap presses with every click and glows in the colour of your switch
[*] Custom packs: drop .ogg / .wav files or a .zip into the sound pack folder
[/list]

[h2]Custom sound packs[/h2]
Open the in-game config, press [b]Open sound pack folder[/b], then add:
[list]
[*] Loose .ogg files (pack name: [i]single[/i])
[*] A folder of .ogg files (the folder name is the pack name)
[*] A .zip of .ogg files (the zip name is the pack name)
[/list]
Press [b]Rescan sound packs[/b] after adding files. Do not use .jar files as sound packs.

Clicks follow the game's Sound volume by default. If they are too quiet, raise game Sound or turn off [b]Follow game Sound volume[/b].
